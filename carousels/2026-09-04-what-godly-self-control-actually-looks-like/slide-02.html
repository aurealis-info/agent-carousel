Step 1

Willpower runs out. A decision doesn't.

You don't lose to the phone at 1 AM because you're weak. You lose because you're negotiating with yourself in the exact moment you have the least leverage.

The strong move happens hours earlier, when nothing's even tempting you yet.

  • Set the boundary at noon, not at midnight.
  • Say it out loud to yourself. A decision you can't quote isn't a decision.
