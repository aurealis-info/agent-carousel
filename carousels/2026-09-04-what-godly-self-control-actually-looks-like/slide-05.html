Step 4

Hand somebody else a key to the door

The guy governing his own impulses all by himself is still one bad night from losing it.

Real self-control has a witness: a brother who gets the text before the scroll, not after.

  • Tell one man your actual trigger, not the sanitized version.
  • Let him ask the follow-up question you're hoping he forgets.

The man who runs his own life is still the man who hands somebody else a key to the door.
