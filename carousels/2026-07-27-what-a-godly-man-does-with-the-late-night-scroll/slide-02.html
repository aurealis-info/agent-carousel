Step 1

Name what you're actually chasing

You don't open the app for the content. You open it for the feeling before the guilt.

Numbness. Noise. Anywhere but the quiet.

  • Ask what you're avoiding, not what you're scrolling.
  • The app is the symptom. Name the ache underneath it.

You can't fight a craving you won't name.
