Step 2

Let him get the credit

He'll fix something you showed him how to fix. The boss will thank him, not you.

Paul trained men who would outlast him: "I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth."

  • Say his name in the meeting when he did the work.
  • Skip the part where you mention you taught him.

A humble man doesn't need his name on it.
