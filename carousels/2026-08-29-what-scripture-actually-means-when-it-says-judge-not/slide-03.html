Step 2

Most men run the order backward

Sharp with her mistakes. Soft on your own sin. That's the flip, and Scripture never taught it that way.

Jesus wept with people who were struggling. He also flipped tables in the temple. Know which one the moment in front of you actually calls for.

  • Get harder on your own excuses.
  • Get gentler with the guy failing exactly like you did last year.
