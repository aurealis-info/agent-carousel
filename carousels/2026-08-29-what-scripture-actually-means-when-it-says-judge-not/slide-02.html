The verse isn't asking you to stay silent

Matthew 7 doesn't tell you to stop noticing sin. It tells you to stop pointing at his before you've dealt with your own.

Read the next line. Take the log out of your own eye first, then you'll see clearly to help him with the speck.

  • Ask what your own life looks like under the standard you just handed him.
