Step 3

Pick a real hour, not a feeling

"When I feel done" isn't a cutoff. It's a permission slip that never expires.

Pick a clock time. 10:30. 11. Then hold it on the boring Tuesday, not just the night you already feel disciplined.

  • A feeling negotiates. A clock doesn't.
  • The boring nights are the ones that actually build it.
