It puts the work under something bigger

Sixty seconds asking God to use the next eight hours turns a job into an offering.

Not motivation. Ownership shifts before the first email even opens.

  • Pray before the first task, not after the first frustration.
  • Ask Him to use the work, not just get you through it.

Work prayed over is work that means something.
