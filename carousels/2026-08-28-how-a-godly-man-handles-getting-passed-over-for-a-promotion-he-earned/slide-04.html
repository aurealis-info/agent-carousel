Step 3

Work the job in front of you like it's the one you wanted

Bitterness makes a man shrink his effort to match his disappointment. Don't let it.

Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for men.

  • Show up fully for the role you have, not the one you didn't get.
  • Let your work outlast your resentment.
