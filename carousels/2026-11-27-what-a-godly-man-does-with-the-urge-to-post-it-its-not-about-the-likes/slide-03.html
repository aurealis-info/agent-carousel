Step 2

Keep some things between you and God

Jesus said pray with the door shut. Give so your left hand doesn't know. Fast without telling anyone.

Not because sharing is wrong. Some things shrink when they get an audience.

  • Pick one habit you never post: your quiet time, your giving.
  • Let God be the only one who sees it.

"Your Father who sees in secret will reward you."
