Let the ache be honest, not despairing

It's fine to want a wife. It's not fine to let the wait convince you God forgot you.

Name the ache to Him instead of numbing it in a feed.

  • Pray it instead of scrolling it.
  • Tell one brother you're struggling with it.

An honest ache still points to God. A buried one points to a scroll.
