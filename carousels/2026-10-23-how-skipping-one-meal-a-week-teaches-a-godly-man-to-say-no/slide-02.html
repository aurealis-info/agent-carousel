Step 1

Pick the meal and tell no one

Jesus said "when you fast," not "if." Then He said to do it without a show (Matthew 6:16-18).

Choose one ordinary meal. Lunch on Wednesday works.

  • No post. No announcement.
  • If your body or your doctor says no, skip this one.

A fast nobody sees is the only kind that trains you.
