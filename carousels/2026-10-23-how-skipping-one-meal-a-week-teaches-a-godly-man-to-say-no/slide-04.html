Notice what argues with you

Around 6 PM your stomach starts making a case.

"I deserve this." "Just a snack." Notice how convincing it sounds.

  • That voice is the one you meet at 1 AM.
  • You don't have to obey it. You only have to hear it.

Your impulses sound sure. That doesn't make them right.
