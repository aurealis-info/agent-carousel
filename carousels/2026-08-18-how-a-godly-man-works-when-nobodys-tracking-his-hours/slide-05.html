Step 4

He lets the work be an offering, not an audition

An audition needs an audience. An offering doesn't check who's in the room.

Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord (Colossians 3:23).

  • Do the good work even when no one will ever know it was you.
  • Let that be enough. It already is to Him.

Nobody has to see it for it to count.
