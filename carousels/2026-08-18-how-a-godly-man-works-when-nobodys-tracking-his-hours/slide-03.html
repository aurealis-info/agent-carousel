Step 2

He shows up on the slow Tuesday, not just the deadline

Anyone can find urgency for a launch. Character shows up for a slow Tuesday with nothing due.

The men who last are the ones who never needed the deadline to work hard.

  • Set the same start time whether it's exciting or not.
  • Let consistency, not adrenaline, run your week.

Discipline is what you do on the boring day.
