Step 4

Measure the wall by Tuesday, not by feeling

You'll know the wall's holding not by how strong you felt, but by what you did the Tuesday nobody was checking.

Self-control shows up in the boring middle of the week.

  • Pick one ordinary Tuesday to track it.
  • Let that day, not Sunday, be the score.

A godly man's walls hold on Tuesday, not just on Sunday.
