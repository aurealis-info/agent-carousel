Step 4

Name the hard thing before it can hide

Write down the one task you'll want to avoid tomorrow. The call, the conversation, the box you keep pushing to Friday.

A named thing can't hide behind "later" until noon.

  • One line, tonight: the thing you'll want to dodge.
  • Put it first on the list, not last.

Decide it tonight, so tomorrow can't talk you out of it.
