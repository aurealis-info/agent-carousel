Step 3

Put the alarm across the room

Whatever's beside your bed is the first thing you reach for.

If that's your phone, your morning belongs to whoever's on the other side of it before it belongs to you.

  • Move the alarm somewhere you have to stand up for it.
  • Standing is the whole trick. Once you're up, you're up.

You can't scroll your way out of a room you're not standing in.
