Step 3

Go first where he did not

Be the first to apologize. Be the first one at the table. Pray out loud before the meal.

Christ went first into the cost. He is also the one who finishes the work in you.

  • Apologize first at the next small fight.
  • Show up early to one thing he would have skipped.

Somebody has to go first. It can start with you.
