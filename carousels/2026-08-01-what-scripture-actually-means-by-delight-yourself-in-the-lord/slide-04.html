Step 3

A wife isn't the finish line

If she's the reason you're chasing God, you're not pursuing him. You're using him.

A walk with God that ends at a ring was never a walk with God. It was a strategy with a verse taped to it.

  • Chase him because he's God, not because she's the prize.
  • A wife is the fruit. She was never the root.
