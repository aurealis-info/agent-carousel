Step 3

Honor isn't the same as obeying

You're a grown man. Some decisions are yours, and some will disappoint them.

Honor means you listen all the way, answer with respect, and never mock them to your friends.

  • Hear them out before you decide.
  • Defend them when they're not in the room.

You can disagree with your father and still honor him.
