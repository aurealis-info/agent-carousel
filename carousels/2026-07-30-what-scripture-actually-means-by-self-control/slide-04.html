Galatians names it last on purpose.

It's the fruit that shows up once everything else is in place. "The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control" (Galatians 5:22-23). It's not something you white-knuckle. It's what's left standing in a man walking with God.
