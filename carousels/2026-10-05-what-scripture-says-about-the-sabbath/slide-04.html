Truth 3

Fill it with God, not a nap

Sleeping in and scrolling all day is just a different kind of busy.

Rest with God in it feels different.

  • Be at church with your attention, not only your body.
  • Read a whole chapter, slowly.
  • Eat one long meal with someone.

Rest with no God in it only gets you ready to be busy again.
