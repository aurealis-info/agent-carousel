Step 4

Give her a direction, not a maybe

Keeping things undefined feels safer. It isn't kinder.

A godly man names where this is going, even when the honest answer costs him comfort.

  • Tell her the truth about where you stand this month, not someday.
  • Let your yes be yes so she isn't stuck guessing.

Follow Christ first. Lead her second. Do it in that order and it'll never be a maybe.
