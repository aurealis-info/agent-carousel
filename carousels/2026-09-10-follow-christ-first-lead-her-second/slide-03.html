Say the hard thing before it becomes a bigger one

Leading isn't staying easy to be around.

It's naming the tension while it's still small enough to name kindly.

  • Say it today, not after it's built a case.
  • Say it to her, not about her to a group chat.

A man who avoids the hard thing isn't protecting her. He's protecting himself.
