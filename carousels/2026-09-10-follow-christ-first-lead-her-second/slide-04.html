Step 3

Point her to God, not to yourself

It feels good to be the one she runs to for everything.

It's also a slow way to become her idol instead of her brother in Christ.

  • Pray with her instead of just for her.
  • Ask what God's been showing her, not just how her day was.

A man secure in Christ isn't threatened by her loving God more than him.
