Step 1

Debt is a master, not just a number

“The borrower is the slave of the lender.” Proverbs 22:7.

Owing isn’t always sin, but every payment is a little of your freedom going somewhere else.

  • Write down who you owe and how much.
  • Look at the total. Don’t dodge it.

You can’t fight a number you won’t look at.
