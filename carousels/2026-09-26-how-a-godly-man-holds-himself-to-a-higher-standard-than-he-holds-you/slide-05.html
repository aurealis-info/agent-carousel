Step 4

The standard he keeps when no one's grading him

You get credit for showing up halfway. That's enough, and he means it when he says so.

He doesn't let himself count halfway as done. Nobody's checking his work. That's exactly why he checks it.

  • Let him get away with less than you'd ever give yourself.
  • Hold your own line even when God's the only one watching.

He'll forgive you before you finish explaining. He won't let himself off that easy.
