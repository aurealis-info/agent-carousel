Step 1

He won't excuse his own missed morning

Miss a morning with God and he tells you it's fine, pick it back up tonight.

Miss his own, and he doesn't call it a bad week. He calls it what it is and gets up earlier tomorrow.

  • Your missed morning gets grace. His gets a plan.
  • He holds his own time with God to a standard he'd never preach at you.

Patient with your slow start. Relentless with his own.
