Step 3

Restore him gently. Watch yourself.

Paul wrote to a church full of guys correcting each other's sin: when you catch a brother in it, restore him. Gently.

Then, in the same verse, he turns the mirror around. Watch yourself, or you'll be tempted the same way.

  • Galatians 6:1: restore him gently, watch yourself closely.
  • The gentleness is his. The vigilance is yours.

One verse, two jobs: soften toward him, sharpen toward yourself.
