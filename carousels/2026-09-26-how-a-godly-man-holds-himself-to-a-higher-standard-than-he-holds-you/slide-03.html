Step 2

Patient with your sin. Ruthless with his own.

If you confess the thing he's fighting too, he doesn't flinch. He prays with you and tells you God isn't finished with you yet.

When it's his own sin in the mirror, he skips the pep talk. He names it and cuts it off that night.

  • Your confession gets patience. His gets a deadline.
  • He'll wait on your progress. He won't wait on his own.

Grace buys you time. It never buys him an excuse.
