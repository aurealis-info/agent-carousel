Step 4

Meet his confession with grace, not a lecture

If confession earns him a lecture, he won't do it again. And neither will you.

Grace is what makes the next brother trust it enough to try.

  • Meet the confession with grace, not shock.
  • That's what makes the next man believe it's safe.

Confess your sins to one another, that you may be healed.
