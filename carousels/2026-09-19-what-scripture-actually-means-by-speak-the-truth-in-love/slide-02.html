Step 1

You default to one lane

Some men only know truth. Blunt, exacting, allergic to excuses. Sharp with everyone, including the guy who needed grace that day.

Some men only know love. Soft on everything, especially the sin sitting right in front of them, because naming it might cost the friendship.

  • Truth without love reads as contempt.
  • Love without truth reads as cowardice.

Scripture never asked you to pick a lane.
