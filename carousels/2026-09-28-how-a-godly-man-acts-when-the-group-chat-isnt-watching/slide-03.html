Step 2

Let your jokes pass the prayer test

A godly man doesn't laugh at what he'd be ashamed to pray about.

If a joke needs you to be someone else, it's not actually funny. It's just permission.

  • Notice the joke you only tell in one room.
  • Ask what it's actually asking you to become.

What makes the group chat laugh should survive the drive home.
