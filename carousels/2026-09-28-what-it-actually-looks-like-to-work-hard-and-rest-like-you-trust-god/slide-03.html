Step 2

Show up on the slow days too

Motivation shows up for the exciting stuff. Obedience shows up on a Tuesday.

A man who only works when he feels it isn't disciplined. He's just moody.

  • Set the alarm the night before, not when you feel ready.
  • Let showing up be the whole win on some days.

Discipline is just obedience with a schedule.
