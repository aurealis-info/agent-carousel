Step 3

Rest isn't a reward you earn

You don't rest because you finally deserve it. You rest because God does, and He made you like Him.

Refusing to stop isn't devotion. It's a man who doesn't trust anyone to hold what he's carrying.

  • Pick one evening this week you actually stop.
  • Let it be unproductive on purpose.

The Sabbath was never about being tired enough to earn it.
