Step 2

Seek Him first, in that order

"Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." Matthew 6:33

A wife sits inside "all these things." Chase her first and she ends up carrying weight only God can.

  • Pick one way you'll chase Him this week
  • A daily chapter, a serving slot, a Sunday you don't skip

The order is the whole thing.
