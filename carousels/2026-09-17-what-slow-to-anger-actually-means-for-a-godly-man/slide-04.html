Step 3

Take it to God before you take it to him

Jesus flipped tables. He also let men mock him in silence. The difference wasn't the emotion. It was who he answered to first.

Say it to God straight before you say it to the guy who set you off.

  • Prayer isn't the polite version of venting.
  • It's where you find out if the anger is righteous or just wounded pride.
