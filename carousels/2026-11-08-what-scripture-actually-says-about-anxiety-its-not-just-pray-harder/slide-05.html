Step 4

Do the next small thing

Anxiety lives in next year. Matthew 6:34 pulls you back to today.

God’s peace guards your mind. Your job is to keep your hands moving.

  • Pick one task you can finish in 20 minutes.
  • If it stays heavy for weeks, tell a brother and see a doctor.

Peace guards the heart. Your hands keep working.
