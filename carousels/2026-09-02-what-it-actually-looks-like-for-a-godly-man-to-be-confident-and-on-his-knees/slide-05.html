Step 4

Kneel before you speak

Before the meeting, before the hard conversation, before you tell anyone what you're sure of, tell God first.

"Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you." — James 4:10

  • Pray before you posture.
  • Let the room see a man who's already been low somewhere else.

Sturdy on his feet. Soft nowhere but his knees.
