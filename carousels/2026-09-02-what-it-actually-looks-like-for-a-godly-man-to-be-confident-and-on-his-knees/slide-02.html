Step 1

Get sure of who you answer to

Real confidence isn't loud. It's a man who already knows whose he is, so the room's opinion stops running the show.

That's a settled thing, not a performed one.

  • Say out loud, once, what you actually believe about yourself.
  • Then stop needing the room to confirm it.

A man secure in Christ has nothing left to prove.
