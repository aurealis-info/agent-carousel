Trust you can't talk your way into

You can talk your way into a room. You cannot talk your way into being trusted with something that matters.

Every task you finish right and every deadline you actually hit is a deposit. Skip enough of them and no one hands you the real weight.

  • Trust is built one finished thing at a time, not one good pitch.

A man's word is worth what his work backs up.
