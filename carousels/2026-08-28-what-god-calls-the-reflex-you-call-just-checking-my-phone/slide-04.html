Step 3

Put a body between you and the reflex

Willpower loses to a reflex nine times out of ten. You won't out-discipline a habit that lives in your hand.

Put the phone in another room, not just on silent, and let the walk back to it slow you down.

  • Leave it somewhere that costs you a few steps to reach.
  • Replace the reach with one line said to God instead.
