Step 2

Ask what you fed first

Whatever you reach for first when you're alone is what's actually training you.

Whoever you feed first wins the day. Most days it isn't even close.

  • Name the first thing your hand went to today.
  • Decide tonight what it'll be tomorrow, before tomorrow decides for you.

You're not out of control. You're just well-trained by the wrong thing.
