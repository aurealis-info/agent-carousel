Step 4

Ask Him to finish what you can't

This isn't a willpower contest you're supposed to win alone.

He who began a good work in you will carry it on. Your nightly question ends there, not with you.

  • Say it out loud before you sleep: I can't. You can.
  • Wake up and do the next small thing anyway.

A governed man still needs Someone governing him.
