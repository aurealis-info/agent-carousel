Step 2

Scripture never mentions a phone. It names the split heart

"You cannot serve two masters" wasn't written about money. It was written about anything that competes for the seat only God's supposed to sit in. Your phone at dinner is just the modern version of the old problem.

  • The table is asking for you. The phone is only asking for your eyes.
  • Whoever gets your full attention is who you're actually serving in that moment.
