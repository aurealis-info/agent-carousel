Step 4

Keep the brother who corrects you close

The friend who only ever agrees with you isn't sharpening you. He's just present.

Proverbs 27:17: iron sharpens iron. Sharpening was never supposed to feel good.

  • Keep the men who tell you the truth.
  • Lose the ones who only tell you what you want to hear.

A godly man can be corrected. That's how you know it's real.
