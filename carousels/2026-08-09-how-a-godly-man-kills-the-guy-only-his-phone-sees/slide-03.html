Step 2

Say the real version to one man

Hidden sin doesn't get weaker with time. It just gets quieter, and quiet isn't the same as gone.

James wrote it plain: confess your sins to one another and pray for each other, so that you may be healed (James 5:16). Not to the group. To one man.

  • Pick the name before you talk yourself out of it.
  • Say what it actually was, not the version that sounds fine out loud.
