Step 1

Call it what it actually is

Procrastination doesn't sound like procrastination. It sounds like wisdom: "I'm waiting for peace about it," "I don't want to rush this."

Most days that's not discernment. That's fear, dressed for church.

  • Ask yourself: are you waiting on God, or waiting on comfort?
  • If you can't tell the difference, you already know the answer.
