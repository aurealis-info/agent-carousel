Truth 4

Train it to say no

Paul didn’t train for looks. He trained so his body wouldn’t run him.

Every hard rep is practice at telling your flesh no.

  • Finish the set when you want to quit.
  • Let that same no show up at 11 PM.

“I discipline my body and keep it under control.” 1 Corinthians 9:27
