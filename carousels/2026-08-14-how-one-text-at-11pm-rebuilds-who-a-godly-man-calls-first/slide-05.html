Step 4

Let him call you out and stay

The first correction tests whether the friendship is real.

James 5:16 says confess to one another so you can be healed, not so you can be managed.

  • Don't go quiet when he says the hard thing.
  • Thank him for saying it, then actually change.

The brother who tells you the truth is the one worth keeping close.
