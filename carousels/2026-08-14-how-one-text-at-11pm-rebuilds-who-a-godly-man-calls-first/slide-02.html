Step 1

Send the text before you need one

Waiting until you're wrecked to reach out isn't humility. It's isolation with a good excuse.

Proverbs says a friend loves at all times, not just the bad ones.

  • Text one brother something true tonight, not just "you good?"
  • Do it before the pressure builds, not after it breaks you.

The men who show up in the crisis are the men you texted before it.
