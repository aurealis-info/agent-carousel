Step 1

Seek the Kingdom before you seek a ring

Matthew 6:33 didn't say seek her first and God gets added to you.

Get the order backward and you're building on sand before you've laid a single brick.

  • Ask what you're actually seeking first this week.
  • Let God be the thing you're building toward, not a wife.

Seek Him first. She was never the finish line.
