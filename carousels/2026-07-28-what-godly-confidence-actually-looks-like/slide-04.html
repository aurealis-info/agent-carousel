Step 3

It kneels without losing itself

The world tells you confidence means never bowing to anyone.

Scripture says the strongest man in the room is the one low enough to kneel.

  • Ask what you're too proud to ask God for.
  • Bring it to Him before you bring it to your own strength.

He can look any man in the eye and still get on his knees.
