Step 1

Stop calling it a struggle

A struggle is something you're still fighting fair. What's actually killing you isn't a struggle, it's an enemy with a foothold. Struggle-language lets you lose slowly and still feel okay about it.

  • Call the thing what it is.
  • Then treat it like a threat, not a quirk.

You don't manage an enemy. You put it down.
