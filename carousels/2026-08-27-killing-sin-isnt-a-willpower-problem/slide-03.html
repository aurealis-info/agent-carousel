Step 2

Cut the supply line

Willpower loses to a phone in your pocket at 1 a.m. every time. The fight isn't won in the moment of temptation, it's won hours earlier, when you decide what has access to you.

  • Remove what feeds it before you're tired.
  • Make the sin harder to reach than the alternative.

Discipline is mostly a decision made in daylight.
