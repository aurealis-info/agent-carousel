Say it out loud to one man

Sin dies in the light and grows in the dark. The version of you that only exists in private is the version that's winning. One honest sentence to a brother does more than another silent promise to yourself.

  • Text one man today, not someday.
  • Let him ask you again next week.

Confess it before it gets to keep you.
