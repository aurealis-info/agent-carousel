Step 4

Go find him after

Standing up in the room is half the job. The other half happens in a text he wasn't expecting.

Tell him what got said. Tell him you shut it down. Don't let him hear it secondhand from someone who wasn't on his side.

The guy who leads isn't the loudest one in the chat. He's the one who followed up after it went quiet.
