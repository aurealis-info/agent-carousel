Step 1

Grieve it honestly

Pretending you're fine isn't strength. David wrote whole Psalms out of a broken heart.

"The Lord is near to the brokenhearted." Psalm 34:18. Near, not disappointed.

  • Tell God exactly how it hurts, out loud or on paper.
  • Don't clean it up first.

He can handle the real version.
