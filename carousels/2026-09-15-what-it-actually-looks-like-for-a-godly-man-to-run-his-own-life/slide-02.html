The urge is not the boss

Anger, the scroll, the second helping, the drink after a hard day. It knocks, sure. It doesn't get to run the house.

A man ruled by his impulses is a man with no walls. Anybody walks in.

  • Notice the urge.
  • Decide anyway.

The urge is a knock, not a key.
