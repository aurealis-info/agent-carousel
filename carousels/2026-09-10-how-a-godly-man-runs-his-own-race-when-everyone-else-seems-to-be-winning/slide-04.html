Step 3

Ask God what your lane actually is

Scripture never tells you to run his race. It tells you to run the one marked out for you.

That means the calling on your life won't look like the calling on his.

  • Pray about your next step, not his last one.
  • Get honest with a brother about what you're actually chasing.

"Let us run with endurance the race that is set before us." Hebrews 12:1
