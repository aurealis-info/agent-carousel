Let his win be proof, not a threat

If God provided for him, that's not evidence you've been forgotten.

It's evidence God actually moves. Let it build your faith instead of your resentment.

  • Text him congratulations before the envy gets to write the message.
  • Let his season remind you yours is still coming.

Run your own race. It's the only one you were ever given to run.
