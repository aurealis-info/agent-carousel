Step 4

Make it standing, not whenever

"Whenever" means never, once the week gets busy. It always gets busy.

A standing hour survives a bad week. A maybe doesn't.

  • Same night, every week, on the calendar like anything else that matters.
  • Show up even on the week you don't feel like it. Especially then.

The men who stay clean aren't the strongest ones. They're the most known ones.
