Step 2

Put the obstacle before the urge

Willpower is a rented mule. It shows up tired most nights.

A godly man doesn't out-white-knuckle his impulses. He puts something slower between himself and the yes: the charger in the other room, the card left at home, the app he actually deleted.

  • Make the wrong thing take three extra steps. Most urges don't survive three steps.
