Step 3

Invite the guy who can't pay you back

Jesus was specific about the guest list.

"When you give a feast, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, the blind." Luke 14:13

  • Write down one name on the edge: the new guy, the one who eats alone.
  • Ask him first, before your usual crew.

The table is for whoever needs a seat.
