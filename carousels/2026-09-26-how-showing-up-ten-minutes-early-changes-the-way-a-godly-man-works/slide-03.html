Step 2

The extra minutes go to what matters

Those ten minutes aren't for your phone. They're for the one task you'd skip if you walked in late.

Read the hard email first. Pray over the meeting you're dreading. Clear your desk before the day clutters it for you.

  • Handle the thing you'd avoid if you were rushed.
  • Ask God to use the work before you touch it.

What you do with those minutes says what you actually think work is for.
