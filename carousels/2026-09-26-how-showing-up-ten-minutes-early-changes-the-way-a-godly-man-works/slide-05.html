Step 4

It's work no one's clocking you in for

Ten minutes early isn't on anyone's calendar. No one's checking. That's exactly the point.

That's what turns it from a performance into worship.

  • The work no one sees is the work that actually shapes you.
  • Ask what those ten minutes would look like if only God saw them, because He's the only one who does.

Whatever you do, work at it with your whole heart, as for the Lord. (Colossians 3:23)
