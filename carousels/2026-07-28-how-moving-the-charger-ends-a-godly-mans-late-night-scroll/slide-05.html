Step 4

Let the night end quiet

A godly man ends his day the way he wants his mind to run while he sleeps, not chasing forty tabs.

Set a hard stop and mean it.

  • Pick a cutoff time and put the phone down at it.
  • Give the last thirty minutes to Scripture, prayer, or actual sleep.

The kitchen charger is a small war. Small wars are still wars.
