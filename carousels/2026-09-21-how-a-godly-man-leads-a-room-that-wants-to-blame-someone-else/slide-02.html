Step 1

Own it out loud, first

The second it goes wrong, say so before anyone has to ask.

Not after they figure it out. Not once someone else takes the fall for it.

  • "That one's on me." No qualifier after it.
  • Say it to the room, not just to God in private.

A man who names his own failure first is the only one in the room nobody's afraid to follow.
