Step 4

Take the hit so he doesn't have to

When the room needs someone to absorb the cost, don't wait to see who volunteers.

Step toward it. Let it land on you instead of the guy who can least afford it.

  • Ask who's most exposed before you ask who's most at fault.

The guy who takes the hit is the guy they'll actually follow into the next one.
