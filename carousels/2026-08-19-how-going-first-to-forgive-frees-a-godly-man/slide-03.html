Forgiving isn't saying it didn't hurt

It's saying the debt doesn't run your life anymore. Two different things.

Pretending it didn't happen isn't forgiveness. It's denial with a Bible verse taped to it.

  • Name what it actually cost you.
  • Then hand the debt to God instead of collecting on it yourself.

You can forgive a man and still not hand him the keys again.
