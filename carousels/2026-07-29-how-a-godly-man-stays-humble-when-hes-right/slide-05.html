Step 4

Kneel where nobody claps

The room forgets who was right by next week. It won't forget who got ugly about it.

God doesn't forget either way. Humility isn't hiding that you were right. It's not needing the room to say so.

"Humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, so that at the proper time he may exalt you." (1 Peter 5:6)

Being right is quiet work. Let God do the loud part.
