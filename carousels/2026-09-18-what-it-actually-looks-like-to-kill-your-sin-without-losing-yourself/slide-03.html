Step 2

Confess it the same day

What you carry alone for a week turns into a secret you're managing. What you say out loud the same day just turns into something you've dealt with.

Speed matters more than the words you use.

  • Take it to God before the sun goes down on it.
  • Then say the sentence out loud to one actual person.

What festers in the dark controls you. What's spoken loses its grip.
