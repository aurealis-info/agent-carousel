Step 3

Track the kill, not the streak

A number on a counter app turns your sin into a game you can lose overnight.

Today's no is the only one you actually get to fight. Fight that one.

  • Ask "did I say no today," not "how many days am I on."
  • A broken streak isn't a broken man. It's one day to go confess and reset.

You're not building a record. You're building a man who says no today.
