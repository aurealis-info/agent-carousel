Step 1

Fight the sin, not the man God's rebuilding

You can hate what you did without hating who you are.

One kills the sin. The other just keeps you circling it.

  • Name the act as what it is. Don't rename yourself over it.
  • Guilt says you did wrong. Shame says you are wrong. Only one is true.

Ruthless with the sin. Patient with the man still being built.
