Step 4

Let a brother see the fight, not just the win

You'll happily tell a friend about the streak. You won't tell him about last Tuesday.

That's the half he actually needs to see.

  • Text him the day it happens, not the month it's over.
  • A man who only shows the win is still performing, even in private.

A fight no one sees is a fight you're fighting alone.
