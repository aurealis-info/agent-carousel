Guarding means gatekeeping

A guard doesn't manage his moods. He decides what gets in.

Your phone is the busiest gate you own, and most guys left it wide open years ago.

  • Ask what you let in without ever deciding to.
  • Notice what you reach for the second you're bored.

You don't drift into godliness. You don't drift into anything good.
