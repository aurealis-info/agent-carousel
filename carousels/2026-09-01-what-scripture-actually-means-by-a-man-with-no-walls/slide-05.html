Step 4

The Spirit lays the stones, not you alone

Self-control is fruit of the Spirit, not a resolution you white-knuckle by yourself (Galatians 5:23).

You don't build the wall by trying harder. You build it by staying close enough to the One who's already laying stone.

  • Ask for it before you white-knuckle it.

A man with walls isn't rigid. He's finally free to open the gate on his own terms.
