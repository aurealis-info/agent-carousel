Step 1

The wall was never willpower

Proverbs 25:28 puts it plainly: a man with no self-control is a city with no walls.

Not weak. Breached. Anybody walks in: the scroll, the second drink, the comment he shouldn't have made.

  • Willpower is a wall you try to rebuild every night from nothing.
  • A wall is a wall because it's already standing before the enemy shows up.
