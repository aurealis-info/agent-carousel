Step 3

Decide before the moment, not during it

You don't have the willpower to out-argue the urge at 11 PM. Nobody does.

So decide at 7 AM. Phone charges in the kitchen. Browser stays logged out. Make the fight smaller before it starts.

  • Willpower loses to a decision made in advance.
  • Build the wall while you're still clear-headed.
