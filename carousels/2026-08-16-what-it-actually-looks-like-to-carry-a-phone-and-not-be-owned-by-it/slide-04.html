Step 3

Let it go quiet when someone's in front of you

A buzzing phone on the table says the guy across from you is the backup plan.

Put it away before the conversation starts, not after it buzzes.

  • Face it down, or better, leave it in your pocket.
  • Give the guy in front of you your whole attention. He'll notice.

Presence is the whole point.
