Step 4

Measure the week by what it cost you

Check your screen time on Sunday. Not to feel bad. To be honest about what actually ran your week.

Then ask what you'd have done with those hours if you'd chased God with them instead.

  • Look at the number without excusing it.
  • Trade one of those hours for Scripture this week.

You don't need a detox. You need a war you're actually fighting.
