Step 3

It kills entitlement

An ungrateful man always thinks he's owed more. More pay, more respect, more rest.

Romans 1 puts not giving thanks right next to a darkened heart. It's not a small sin. (Romans 1:21)

  • Find where you complain the most. Thank God there first.
  • Thank the person who serves you, by name.

You can't feel owed and thankful at the same time.
