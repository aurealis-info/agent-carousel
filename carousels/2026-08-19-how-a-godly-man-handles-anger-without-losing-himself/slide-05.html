Step 4

Point it at the problem, not the person

Righteous anger has a target: what's wrong. It's never the man standing in front of you.

Ephesians says be angry and don't sin. Anger and sin aren't the same thing. Only one of them is optional.

  • Ask what the anger wants fixed.
  • Fix that. Don't just discharge the feeling.

A godly man's anger builds something. It doesn't just burn something down.
