Step 4

Use it or it uses you

The same anger that could wreck your night can also tell you what you actually care about. Jesus flipped tables over what was happening in His Father's house. That's anger aimed somewhere.

  • Ask what your anger is protecting.
  • Point it there instead of at whoever's closest.

A godly man doesn't kill his anger. He puts it under orders.
