Step 4

Learn contentment again

Under every bet is the same whisper: what God gave you isn't enough.

Paul said he learned to be content. Learned. It took practice.

  • Give the money you'd have bet to someone in need.
  • Watch the game for the game. Thank God for the free Saturday.

Godliness with contentment is great gain. That's the only sure thing on the board.
