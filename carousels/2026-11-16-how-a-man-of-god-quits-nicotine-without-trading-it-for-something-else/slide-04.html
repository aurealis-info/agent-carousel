Step 3

Ride the craving out with God

A craving peaks and fades in minutes. You don't have to beat it forever. Just this one.

Use those minutes. Pray short and honest: God, I want it. Help me want You more.

  • Walk outside. Drink cold water. Pray.
  • Wait ten minutes before you decide anything.

Every craving you ride out gets weaker.
