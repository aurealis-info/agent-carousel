Step 1

You can hear "you're wrong" and keep your footing

An insecure man defends himself before he's even finished hearing the sentence.

A man sure of who he is in Christ can actually sit with it, because his worth was never riding on being right.

  • Let the criticism finish before you respond.
  • Ask what he saw that you didn't, before you explain yourself.
