Notice what you're avoiding

The task you keep pushing to tomorrow is rarely the hardest one on your list. It's just the most boring. Boring doesn't feel urgent, so it's easy to let it sit while you do something that feels productive instead.

  • Name the task you've moved three days in a row.
  • That's the one to start with tomorrow.

Avoidance always wears a productive disguise.
