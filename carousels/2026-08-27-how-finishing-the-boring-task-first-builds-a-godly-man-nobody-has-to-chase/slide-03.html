Step 2

Do it first, not when you feel like it

Motivation shows up after you start, not before. A man who waits to feel like doing the boring thing will wait a long time. Put it first on the list, before your energy has a vote.

  • First hour, hardest boring thing.
  • Let the rest of the day be the reward.

You don't feel your way into discipline. You do your way into it.
