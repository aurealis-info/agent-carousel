Step 4

Let it build the reputation you actually want

Nobody remembers the flashy thing you did once. They remember whether the boring thing got done every time, without a reminder. That's the difference between a man people admire and a man people can actually build with.

  • Reliability is boring work, repeated.
  • That's the whole reputation.

The boring work is the resume nobody reads and everybody trusts.
