What You Hide Gets to Stay

Every time you keep it to yourself, you're not protecting anyone. You're feeding it the one thing it needs: nobody else knowing it's there.

Proverbs said it plainly a long time before phones existed. Whoever conceals their sins does not prosper, but the one who confesses and renounces them finds mercy. (Proverbs 28:13)
