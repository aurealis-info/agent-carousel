Give him the excuse you never give yourself

You know exactly why you snapped, why you're behind, why you're still stuck.

Give him that same benefit before you judge him for the same thing.

  • Assume the hard week before you assume the bad character.
  • Ask what you'd want said about you on your worst day.

Grace for him. Honesty for you.
