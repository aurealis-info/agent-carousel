Step 4

Hand him a standing question

One text, same time every week: "Clean this week? Yes or no."

He can't help with what he doesn't know, and you can't hide from a question that keeps coming.

  • Answer the same day, even when it's a no
  • A no told fast beats a yes told late

You don't have to carry it in the dark anymore.
