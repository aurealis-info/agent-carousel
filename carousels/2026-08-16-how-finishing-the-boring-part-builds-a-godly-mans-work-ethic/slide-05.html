Step 4

Let God grade the work no one sees

You'll do a hundred things this year nobody claps for. That's not wasted work.

That's the work that's actually forming you.

  • Pick one unseen task and do it like He's watching. He is.
  • Let the applause you don't get be the point, not the miss.

What nobody sees is exactly what's being weighed.
