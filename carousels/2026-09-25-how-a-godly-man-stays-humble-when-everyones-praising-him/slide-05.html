Step 4

Take the correction without the defense

A humble man can be told he's wrong and still stand up straight.

Pride flinches. Humility just says thank you and adjusts.

  • Let the criticism land before you explain yourself.
  • The men who can't be corrected stop growing right when they got good.

He can look any man in the eye and still get on his knees.
