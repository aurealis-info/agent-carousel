The body trains the soul

Paul said, "I discipline my body and keep it under control." 1 Corinthians 9:27

Every small no to the snooze, the third plate, the late scroll is a rep for the bigger no you'll need later.

  • Pick one bodily no this week and keep it.
  • Tell a brother which one you picked.

Win the small fights. The big ones follow.
