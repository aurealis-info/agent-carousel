Truth 1

It's not yours to waste

Most guys only hear this verse in a talk about lust. Paul meant everything the body does.

"You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body." 1 Corinthians 6:19–20

  • Sleep, food, and movement are stewardship.
  • Not a hobby. Not a vanity project.

Bought with a price. Treat it that way.
