Step 4

Let the relief prove it's working

You'll feel lighter before you feel different. That's not nothing.

God finishes what He starts in you. Your job is to keep showing up to the fight.

  • Notice the morning you didn't reach for it first.
  • Thank Him for that morning before you forget it happened.

The relief is Him working. Let it be proof, not permission to stop.
