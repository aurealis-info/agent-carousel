Step 4

The plank is always yours first

Jesus wept over people. He also flipped tables. He never once excused his own sin to make room to point at someone else's.

"Why do you look at the speck of sawdust in your brother's eye and pay no attention to the plank in your own eye?" (Matthew 7:3)

Hard on the sin in the mirror. Gentle with the man across the table.
