Step 3

Close the lonely hours with God, not a search bar

The late-night ache is real. What you feed it decides what it grows into.

Prayer doesn't erase the loneliness. It puts it in the right hands.

  • Name the ache to God before you numb it or chase it.
  • Let silence with Him be the first answer, not the last resort.
