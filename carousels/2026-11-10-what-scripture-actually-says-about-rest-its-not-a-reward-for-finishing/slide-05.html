Step 4

Rest like you trust Him

Mark 2:27 says the Sabbath was made for man. It's a gift, not a test.

Stopping is a way of saying God runs the world and you don't.

  • Do one thing that feeds you: a walk, a meal with brothers, a nap.
  • Let the unfinished list stay unfinished.

God keeps the world running. You're allowed to stop.
